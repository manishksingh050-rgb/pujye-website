Why We Started

Devotion deserves better ingredients.

Growing up, incense was lit every single morning in our home — before school, before work, before anything else. Somewhere along the way, we started noticing the smoke was harsher than we remembered, the packaging felt like an afterthought, and no one could really tell us what was in the stick we were burning next to our gods. That question became Pujye.

Origin

A shared ritual, a shared question

Two siblings, one daily altar, and a growing discomfort with what mass-market incense had quietly become — heavy smoke, synthetic binders, and packaging no one thought twice about.

Approach

Clean formulations, honest sourcing

We set out to build charcoal-free, essential-oil-based incense and dhoop — verified sourcing, standardized quality, and packaging sturdy enough to survive transit without losing its purpose.

Today

Pujye, built from Jhansi

What started as a family conversation is now a brand — rolled, packed, and shipped from Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh, built by the same two people who noticed the problem in the first place.
